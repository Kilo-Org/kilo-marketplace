# Data and measurement

Use this reference when SEO work touches analytics, advertising, consent, logs, user input, search properties, or third-party scripts.

## Separate evidence scopes

- Search-property data describes how that search platform reports discovery, indexing, impressions, clicks, and related dimensions.
- Analytics describes allowed on-site behavior after a visit, subject to its consent, filtering, attribution, and sampling model.
- Laboratory performance describes a controlled run, not real-user field performance.
- Advertising status and revenue are separate from search quality and analytics installation.
- Source or browser checks prove implementation behavior, not delayed external outcomes.

Do not merge these scopes into one success claim.

## Data minimization

Before adding or changing measurement, record the exact allowed pages, events, fields, destinations, consent state, retention, and prohibited data. Default to the smallest useful data set.

Do not send secrets, authentication data, private messages, mailbox content, one-time codes, API keys, tokens, attachments, form or tool input, complete query strings, or unnecessary identifiers to analytics, advertising, ordinary logs, reports, or third parties.

SEO does not justify weakening consent, loading analytics on sensitive pages, adding advertising near high-frequency controls, or making core content depend on consent or ad delivery.

## Verification

Check both positive and negative contracts: approved pages and events behave as intended, while excluded pages, inputs, and fields produce no prohibited request or record. A script's presence is not proof that its data contract is correct.
