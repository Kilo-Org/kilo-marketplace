# Copy and proof

Write for the visitor's decision and awareness level. Keep the traffic promise
consistent through headline, explanation, CTA, form, and confirmation.

## Draft the full journey

1. State the useful outcome and make the audience or use case apparent.
2. Explain the mechanism concretely enough to distinguish the offer.
3. Show evidence where available: real product views, scoped demonstrations,
   permissioned quotes, or clearly sourced results.
4. Resolve the objections that could reasonably stop this audience.
5. Make the action and its commitment explicit: request, download, join, or book.
6. Write field hints, validation, pending, error, retry, success, and next-step copy.

Do not impose a universal section order or word count. An unfamiliar, expensive
offer may need more explanation than a familiar local service. Above-the-fold
clarity matters more than forcing every element above an arbitrary viewport line.

## Maintain a claim record

For each material claim, record its source, owner, date/context, permission when
relevant, and status: supported, needs confirmation, or omitted. Quantified
benefits need denominator, population, and conditions. Do not extrapolate one
case study into a universal promise.

With no customers, show how the product works, a sample deliverable, founder
expertise if provided, or transparent early-stage status. Do not fill an empty
testimonial row with fictional quotes or “trusted by” logos. Demo businesses may
have explicitly fictional premises, but cannot pose as real client results.

Use scarcity, deadlines, guarantees, and response-time promises only when supplied
and supportable. A stylish counter is still misleading if no real limit exists.

## Edit for specificity

- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs over “revolutionary,” “seamless,” and vague value.
- Replace unsupported superlatives with a useful explanation or example.
- Preserve technical detail when it helps this audience decide.
- Use CTA labels that accurately describe the next step and any commitment.
- Make email signup distinct from permission for unrelated marketing.
- Check long text, translated labels, currency, date, and time-zone assumptions.

For a headline-only task, supply the requested copy and a brief rationale grounded
in known facts. Do not load form tooling or manufacture a full redesign.

## Check

A visitor should be able to explain the offer, relevant audience, next step, and
commitment in their own words. Have a person try that when possible. Do not claim
a readability score or an AI-text detector establishes persuasive quality.
